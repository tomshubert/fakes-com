Viral Fakes

Fake photos, AI videos and false rumors going around now, each checked by an established fact-checker. Tap any card to see the fake, safely labeled, at the fact-checker's site.

Image: Lead Stories Fake AI video · Nov 12

Saluting veteran at a Veterans Day parade

A video of a scarred veteran being saluted at a parade was shared as real. Lead Stories found it was made with AI.

The clue: Drumsticks vanish, medals melt into the uniform and the flag's stripes change width.

Image: Lead Stories Fake AI photo · Nov 6

Colosseum and Eiffel Tower lit blue for the midterms

Photos claimed the Colosseum and Eiffel Tower were lit blue for Democratic midterm wins. Lead Stories found they were made with AI.

The clue: Neither landmark's own website or social media showed any such lighting.

Misleading Old video, new claim · Nov 5

Pennsylvania voter registration "scheme" video

A post said a video proved 2026 voter registration fraud in Pennsylvania. Lead Stories found it was a 2024 press conference about the previous election.

The clue: Check when a video was first posted. Old clips with new captions are a common trick.

See it at Lead Stories →
Image: Lead Stories Fake AI-edited photo · Nov 2

Sharp photo of the Florida jet crash

A crisp photo of the Florida jet crash seemed to show a missing wing. Lead Stories found it was apparently dashcam video "cleaned up" with AI.

The clue: It looked sharper than any real footage, and the real video shows both wings.

Image: AP via PolitiFact Real Real video called fake · Nov 2

Florida jet crash video is real, not AI

A post claimed dashcam video of the Florida jet crash was "100%" AI. PolitiFact found it was real and confirmed by major news outlets.

The clue: Calling something "AI" can be false too. Check whether trusted news outlets confirmed it.
