I built Fakes.com because I spend my working days watching good, careful people almost lose money to an email, a phone call or a website that looked completely real.

I'm an IT consultant. I support small businesses, mostly 50 people or fewer, with everything from their computers and networks to their email security. A big part of that job now is stopping scams before they work: a fake invoice, a phony login page, a call from "the bank." This site is the advice I give my clients, written for everyone.

My background

  • I started working with computers in 8th grade, at 13.
  • After graduating from Emory University, I joined The Coca-Cola Company in Atlanta as a special-projects mainframe programmer.
  • In 1984 I co-founded a microcomputer consulting company that built PCs and set up office networks, and became a Novell Direct Gold Reseller.
  • I later moved to Los Angeles and ran the computer systems for a securities firm.
  • Since 1994 I've worked as an independent IT consultant, supporting small businesses and individuals, and I also founded a web services business, YourWEB.

Today I provide 24/7 security monitoring for clients' Microsoft 365 accounts. When something suspicious happens, like a sign-in from another country, I get an alert and can lock the account before damage is done.

An employee at a law firm I support had no idea someone in Germany had just signed into their email. Our monitoring flagged the login right away, and I locked the account before any firm or client data was compromised.
