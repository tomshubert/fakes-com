How we source

  • Original sources first. Figures link to the agency or organization that produced them, such as the FTC, the FBI's Internet Crime Complaint Center or a state attorney general, rather than to articles about them.
  • When a news report is the only source. Some warnings, especially from local police, appear only in local news. In those cases we link the news story and name the agency it quotes.
  • Dates on everything. Each alert shows the date of the warning it's based on.
  • Reported losses are a floor, not a total. FTC and FBI figures count only what people reported. Both agencies say real losses are higher.

Examples and practice messages

Sample scam messages in our guides and in Spot the Fake are written by us for teaching. Web addresses in them use the reserved ending .example so they can never lead to a real scam site, and phone numbers use the 555 range set aside for fiction.

Independence

Fakes.com never sells, promotes or links to counterfeit products. If we ever earn money from advertising or from recommending a product, we'll say so clearly. See our advertising and affiliate disclosure.
