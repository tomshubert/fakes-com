The short version

Fakes.com doesn't ask you to create an account, doesn't sell personal information, and currently doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies.

What we collect

  • Basic visit information. Our hosting and security provider, Cloudflare, processes technical information such as IP addresses and browser type to deliver the site and protect it from attacks. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Spot the Fake. The game runs entirely in your browser. Your answers are not sent to us.
  • Email sign-ups. Our weekly email is not live yet. When it launches, we'll collect only the email address you give us, use it only to send that email, and include an unsubscribe link in every message.

Changes

If we add advertising, visitor statistics or other services that collect information, we'll update this policy before they go live and change the date above.
