The short version

Fakes.com doesn't ask you to create an account, doesn't sell personal information, and currently doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. Service providers (Cloudflare, Google Fonts, and Anthropic if you use the checker) receive information as described below.

What we collect

  • Basic visit information. Our hosting and security provider, Cloudflare, processes technical information such as IP addresses and browser type to deliver the site and protect it from attacks. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Fonts. Our typefaces load from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google's servers, which receive your IP address and browser details. See Google's privacy policy.
  • The "Is it fake?" checker. What you paste or upload is sent to Anthropic, the company that makes the Claude AI, to analyze, and the answer is shown to you. Fakes.com does not store your submission. Anthropic handles it under its commercial terms; see Anthropic's privacy policy. For fact-checks, the AI also searches the web for sources about the claim you submitted. Please don't include passwords, full account numbers or Social Security numbers.
  • Spot the Fake. The game runs entirely in your browser. Your answers are not sent to us.
  • Email sign-ups. Our weekly email is not live yet. When it launches, we'll collect only the email address you give us, use it only to send that email, and include an unsubscribe link in every message.

Changes

If we add advertising, visitor statistics or other services that collect information, we'll update this policy before they go live and change the date above.
