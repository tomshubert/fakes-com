How Fakes.com may earn money, and how that is kept separate from our advice.

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Fakes.com is free to read. To keep it that way, we may earn money in the future from advertising and from affiliate links, which pay us a commission if you buy a product after clicking our link. It never costs you more.

Our rules

  • Any page with affiliate links will say so at the top.
  • We recommend a product only if we would recommend it without the commission.
  • Advertisers do not influence our alerts, guides or recommendations.

Right now, Fakes.com has no ads and no affiliate links. We'll update this page when that changes.
