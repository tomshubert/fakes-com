Sources, Standards & Corrections

How Fakes.com sources its facts, chooses what to publish and corrects mistakes.

How we source

  • Original sources first. Figures link to the agency or organization that produced them, such as the FTC, the FBI's Internet Crime Complaint Center or a state attorney general, rather than to articles about them.
  • When a news report is the only source. Some warnings, especially from local police, appear only in local news. In those cases we link the news story and name the agency it quotes.
  • Dates on everything. Each alert shows the date of the warning it's based on.
  • Reported losses are a floor, not a total. FTC and FBI figures count only what people reported. The FTC says real losses are far higher, and the FBI notes that some crimes go under-reported.

Examples and practice messages

Sample scam messages in our guides and in Spot the Fake are written by us for teaching. Web addresses in them use the reserved ending .example so they can never lead to a real scam site, and phone numbers use the 555-01xx range set aside for fiction.

Independence

Fakes.com never sells, promotes or links to counterfeit products. If we ever earn money from advertising or from recommending a product, we'll say so clearly. See our advertising and affiliate disclosure.

Corrections

If we get something wrong, we fix it and note the correction at the bottom of the page with the date. To report an error, see the contact page.
