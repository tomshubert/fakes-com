Report a scam to the authorities

We can't recover money or investigate cases, but these agencies can act on your report:

  • Federal Trade Commission: ReportFraud.ftc.gov for scams, fraud and bad business practices.
  • FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center: IC3.gov for online crime, especially if you lost money.
  • Identity theft: IdentityTheft.gov for a step-by-step recovery plan.
  • Your bank or card company: call the number on the back of your card right away if you sent money or shared account details.
