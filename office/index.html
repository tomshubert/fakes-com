FAKES.COM for offices

Pilot

Check a suspicious email with one click in Outlook

Fakes.com for Offices adds a Check with Fakes button to Outlook. Open an email that looks off, click the button, and a panel tells you in about 20 seconds whether it looks like a scam, why, and what to do.

How it works

  1. Your IT person turns it on for your organization. You sign in with your normal work Microsoft account; there's no new password.
  2. Open a suspicious email and click Check with Fakes on the ribbon (in some versions it's under Apps or the … menu).
  3. Read the verdict. If it's a scam, delete it. Your IT person is told automatically, so you don't need to forward it.

Privacy

  • The email is checked and then discarded. Fakes.com keeps only a count of how many emails each person checked, for billing.
  • Fakes.com asks Microsoft only for your name and email address. It can't read your mailbox; it only sees the one email you choose to check.

This is a pilot with a small number of offices. Questions: hello@fakes.com. Anyone can still check messages for free at fakes.com/check.
