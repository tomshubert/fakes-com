Scam Alerts

Scams going around right now, based on warnings from government agencies and police. Each alert says what to do and links to the original warning.

EmailTextNov 9

"Unpaid toll" messages are back

The Maine Turnpike Authority warned E-ZPass customers about emails demanding immediate payment for unpaid tolls. The authority has said it does not use text messages to collect tolls, and that violation notices are sent by U.S. Mail.

What to do: Don't click the link. Check your balance by typing your toll agency's web address yourself or calling the number on your account statement.
Source: The Pulse of NH (Nov 9, 2026) · Maine Turnpike Authority
VeteransPhoneNov 9

Scams aimed at service members and veterans

New York's attorney general published a guide to scams that target the military community, including fake "claims help" that charges for VA benefits, pension buyouts, overpriced financing sold per pay period, and fake charities.

What to do: Get help with VA claims only from VA-accredited representatives, and check any benefits message by logging in to your official account directly.
Source: New York Attorney General: Scams targeting the military
PhoneBusinessNov 7

Callers pressuring employees to use Bitcoin ATMs

Police in Ocean City, Md., say scammers are calling hotels, complimenting staff and offering a "bonus" for settling a supposed balance with cash from the register or safe through a Bitcoin ATM.

What to do: Tell staff that no real owner, manager or supplier will ever ask for this. Hang up and call the manager on a known number.
Source: CoastTV, reporting the Ocean City Police Department warning
DonationsTextOct 15

Fake campaign "investments" and scam PACs

The FBI warned that scammers are using the midterm elections to run fake campaign investment pools, pose as political action committees, sell merchandise that never ships, and send fake voter registration messages. Some use AI-generated voices of candidates.

What to do: Donations are never investments. Check a committee on the FEC website before giving, and verify your registration at vote.gov.
Source: FBI IC3 Public Service Announcement I-101526-3-PSA
