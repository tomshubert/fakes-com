Scam Alerts

Scams going around right now, based on warnings from government agencies and police. Each alert says what to do and links to the original warning.

VeteransPhoneNov 9

Scams aimed at service members and veterans

New York's attorney general published a guide to scams that target the military community, including people who charge veterans to "help" with VA claims, offers of quick cash in exchange for pension payments, overpriced financing sold per pay period, and fake charities.

What to do: Don't pay anyone to help file your first VA claim, and check any benefits message by logging in to your official account directly.
Source: New York Attorney General: Scams targeting the military · Yahoo News, reporting the NY Attorney General's new guide (Nov 9, 2026)
PhoneBusinessNov 7

Callers pressuring employees to use Bitcoin ATMs

Police in Ocean City, Md., say scammers are calling hotels, complimenting staff and offering a "bonus" for settling a supposed balance with cash from the register or safe through a Bitcoin ATM.

What to do: Tell staff that the business owner will never ask them to pay a balance with cash from the register or safe through a Bitcoin ATM. Report any such call to police.
Source: CoastTV, reporting the Ocean City Police Department warning
DonationsTextOct 15

Fake campaign "investments" and scam PACs

The FBI warned that scammers are using the midterm elections to run fake campaign investment pools, pose as political action committees, sell merchandise that never ships, and send fake voter registration messages. Some use AI-generated voices of candidates.

What to do: Donations are never investments. Check a committee on the Federal Election Commission's website before giving, and confirm your registration on the official federal voter registration site.
Source: FBI IC3 Public Service Announcement I-101526-3-PSA
